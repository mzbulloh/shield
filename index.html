Market

We start in Taipei. Big city, big impact.

Taipei gives SHIELD a dense, visible proving ground where better issue reporting can improve response quality and reduce manual inspection burden.

Manual inspection can cost about NT$5,000 per kilometer. SHIELD helps government reduce wasted field effort by turning citizen reports into better-targeted, evidence-based cases.
8.7%share of Taipei included value needed for the first 3-month target
Taipei is large enough to prove demand, measurable outcomes, and a strong first reference case.

Big city

Taipei has enough density, infrastructure activity, and public visibility to make early adoption noticeable and measurable.

Big impact

A focused Taipei deployment can show faster reporting, clearer prioritization, and lower dependence on broad manual inspection routines.

Budget value

When manual inspection costs roughly NT$5,000 per kilometer, more targeted case routing becomes a strong public-sector cost-saving story.
